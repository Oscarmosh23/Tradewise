⚡ Scalping-Specific Risk Rules
01
Risk 0.5% max per scalp — not 1-2%
Scalping involves more trades per session than swing trading. At 10 trades/day, 1% risk per trade = 10% daily risk. Use 0.5% maximum. Small consistent gains compound fast.
02
Tight stops — 5 to 15 pips maximum
Scalping requires tight stops. XAUUSD: 8-12 pips. EURUSD: 5-8 pips. BTC: $200-$500. If your stop is wider than this it is not a scalp — it is a swing trade.
03
Only scalp during high-volume sessions
London (8am-4pm GMT) and New York (1pm-9pm GMT) sessions only. Asian session has wide spreads and thin volume — scalping signals are unreliable. Check Sessions tab.
04
Stop after 3 consecutive losses
Three losses in a row means conditions have changed or you are in a choppy market. Stop trading for 30 minutes minimum. Let the market settle before re-entering.
05
Daily loss limit — 2% maximum
If you lose 2% in one session, stop for the day. No exceptions. Revenge trading after a bad session is the fastest way to blow an account.

1:00 PM — 9:00 PM GMT · Best for: XAUUSD, SPY, TSLA, EURUSD
US stock market opens at 1:30 PM GMT. First 90 minutes are the most volatile — best for stock scalping. XAUUSD very active. Forex thins out after 5 PM GMT.

1:00 PM — 4:00 PM GMT · BEST SCALPING WINDOW
The London/New York overlap is the single best window for scalping. All major pairs at maximum liquidity. Tight spreads. Strong directional moves. XAUUSD signals are most reliable here.
ASIAN
11:00 PM — 8:00 AM GMT · AVOID SCALPING
Low volume, wide spreads, choppy price action. Scalping signals have much lower accuracy during Asian session. Only trade JPY pairs if you scalp during this window.

24/7 · Best during London+NY overlap
Crypto trades all day but volume peaks during London and New York sessions. BTC and ETH scalping signals are most reliable 8 AM — 9 PM GMT. Avoid 2 AM — 7 AM GMT.

🔧 Execute Scalping Signals on MT4
01
Set chart to 5-minute timeframe
Open your asset on MT4. Right-click the chart → Timeframe → M5. This matches the TradeWise 5-minute signal. Always trade on the same timeframe as the signal.
Chart → M5 timeframe
02
Add EMA 8 and EMA 21 to your chart
Insert → Indicators → Trend → Moving Average. Add EMA period 8 (blue) and EMA period 21 (red). When blue crosses above red = BUY signal. When blue crosses below red = SELL signal. This is your visual confirmation of the TradeWise signal.
Insert → MA → EMA 8 + EMA 21
03
Wait for TradeWise signal + MT4 visual confirmation
Only enter when TradeWise shows BUY AND you see the EMA crossover on your MT4 chart. Two independent confirmations = higher probability trade. Never trade on one signal alone.
TradeWise signal + EMA cross = ENTER
04
Place order with stop loss and take profit
F9 → New Order → enter volume from Risk Calc tab → set Stop Loss 8-12 pips below entry for XAUUSD → set Take Profit 16-24 pips above entry (minimum 2:1). Click Buy or Sell.
F9 → set SL + TP → Buy/Sell
05
Move stop to breakeven after +10 pips
Once price moves 10 pips in your favor, move stop loss to your entry price. Now you cannot lose money on this trade. This is called a "free trade" and it is the most important scalping skill.
+10 pips → move SL to entry
06
Auto-execution EA code
Paste this into MT4 MetaEditor (F4) to auto-execute TradeWise signals. Change the symbol and risk settings to match your account.
// TradeWise Scalper EA — MT4
extern string Symbol_EA = "XAUUSD";
extern double RiskPercent = 0.5;
extern int StopPips = 10;
extern int TargetPips = 20;
extern int MagicNumber = 20260101;

void OnTick() {
  if(OrdersTotal() > 0) return; // one trade at a time
  double ema8 = iMA(Symbol_EA,PERIOD_M5,8,0,MODE_EMA,PRICE_CLOSE,0);
  double ema21 = iMA(Symbol_EA,PERIOD_M5,21,0,MODE_EMA,PRICE_CLOSE,0);
  double ema8_prev = iMA(Symbol_EA,PERIOD_M5,8,0,MODE_EMA,PRICE_CLOSE,1);
  double ema21_prev = iMA(Symbol_EA,PERIOD_M5,21,0,MODE_EMA,PRICE_CLOSE,1);
  double rsi = iRSI(Symbol_EA,PERIOD_M5,14,PRICE_CLOSE,0);

  // BUY: EMA cross up + RSI < 60
  if(ema8_prev < ema21_prev && ema8 > ema21 && rsi < 60) {
    double price = Ask;
    double sl = price - StopPips * Point * 10;
    double tp = price + TargetPips * Point * 10;
    double lots = CalcLots(RiskPercent, StopPips);
    OrderSend(Symbol_EA,OP_BUY,lots,price,3,sl,tp,"TW",MagicNumber,0,clrGreen);
  }
  // SELL: EMA cross down + RSI > 40
  if(ema8_prev > ema21_prev && ema8 < ema21 && rsi > 40) {
    double price = Bid;
    double sl = price + StopPips * Point * 10;
    double tp = price - TargetPips * Point * 10;
    double lots = CalcLots(RiskPercent, StopPips);
    OrderSend(Symbol_EA,OP_SELL,lots,price,3,sl,tp,"TW",MagicNumber,0,clrRed);
  }
}

double CalcLots(double riskPct, int stopPips) {
  double riskAmt = AccountBalance() * riskPct / 100;
  double pipVal = MarketInfo(Symbol_EA, MODE_TICKVALUE) * 10;
  return NormalizeDouble(riskAmt / (stopPips * pipVal), 2);
}
