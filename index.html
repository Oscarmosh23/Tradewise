📋 Professional Risk Rules
01
Never risk more than 1-2% per trade
$10,000 account = max $100-$200 loss per trade. 10 losing trades in a row = only 10-20% drawdown. Most traders blow accounts risking 10-20% per trade.
02
Always set stop loss before entering
Never move a stop further away to avoid being stopped out. That is how small losses become catastrophic ones.
03
Minimum 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio
If you risk $100 your target must be at least $200. You can be wrong 50% of the time and still break even. 60% win rate + 2:1 RR = very profitable.
04
Never trade during major news events
NFP, Fed decisions, CPI, earnings — check economic calendar before every trade. News events invalidate all technical signals instantly.
🔧 Execute Signals on MT4
01
Find your asset in Market Watch
Press Ctrl+M to open Market Watch. Search for the asset: XAUUSD for gold, BTCUSD for Bitcoin, or the stock ticker.
Ctrl+M → search asset
02
Open new order (F9)
Press F9 or right-click chart → Trading → New Order. You will see Volume, Stop Loss, and Take Profit fields.
F9 → New Order
03
Set volume using Risk Manager
Use the Risk Manager tab to calculate your exact position size. Enter that number in the Volume field. For XAUUSD — 1 lot = 100oz of gold.
Volume = calculated position size
04
Set Stop Loss and Take Profit
Always set both before clicking Buy or Sell. Use the Stop Loss and Target prices from the signal card on the Signals tab.
SL + TP required — no exceptions
05
Click Buy or Sell
BUY = you think price goes UP. SELL = you think price goes DOWN. The signal tells you which. Monitor in Terminal panel (Ctrl+T).
Ctrl+T → monitor open trades
⟠ Execute on Coinbase
01
Use Coinbase Advanced Trade for crypto
Go to advanced.coinbase.com. Set a Limit Order at the entry price. Set a Stop Limit order at the stop loss price from the signal card.
02
Coinbase is crypto only
For stocks use a stock broker (Robinhood, Interactive Brokers). For XAUUSD use MT4 with a forex broker (XM, IC Markets, OANDA).
